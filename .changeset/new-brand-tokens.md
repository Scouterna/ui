Replace the design tokens with the new graphical profile, imported from Penpot. All semantic color names have changed (for example `--color-text-primary`, `--color-bg-surface` and `--color-action-default-solid`), as have the font tokens (`--font-family-body` and `--font-family-heading`, using Inter and Season Mix), and spacing, sizes, radii, shadows and typography now follow the new naming. Typography tokens are `--typography-*` font shorthands. The age-group colors are gone. Fonts are loaded from `https://cdn.scouterna.net/fonts/fonts.css`.
