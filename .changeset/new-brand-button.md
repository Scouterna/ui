Restyle `scout-button` for the new graphical profile. The `variant` prop is replaced by `kind` (`primary`, `secondary`, `tertiary`) and `tone` (`default`, `inverse`, `destructive`). Sizes are now `sm`, `md` and `lg`. The new `shape` prop adds `cut` and `arrow` buttons. `icon` and `iconPosition` are replaced by `iconStart` and `iconEnd`. The `caution` variant is removed.
